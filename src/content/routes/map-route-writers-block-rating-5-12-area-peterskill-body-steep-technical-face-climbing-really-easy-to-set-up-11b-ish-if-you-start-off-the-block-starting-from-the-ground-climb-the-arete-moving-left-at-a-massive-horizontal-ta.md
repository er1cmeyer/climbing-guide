Steep, technical face climbing, really easy to set up. 11b-ish if you start off the block. Starting from the ground, climb the arete, moving left at a massive horizontal. Take a breath, climbing good crimps and trending right to the next horizontal. Move left, climb the shallow left facing corner, again moving right back to the arete. 

## Gear

Small purple, green and the big gray #4 or purple cam #5 for directionals - the gray cam goes in above lower crux. 

## Anchor

Takes nearly everything from big cam to large nuts. 

> Note: top edge of the climb is sharp. Make sure to protect toprope gear from abrasion.
